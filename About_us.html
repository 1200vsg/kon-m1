About eFootball

Welcome to the KONAMI Server eFootball information hub. This page introduces the game, its players and the skills used to build a competitive team.

The Game

eFootball allows players to create football teams and compete in different matches.
A successful squad requires good players, individual skill and teamwork. Build your dream squad and compete.

Player Development

Players can be selected according to their position, attributes and preferred playing style.

Football Positions

Goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards each have different responsibilities.

Team Chemistry

Good combinations between players can make a team more effective.

Database Note

The player information displayed on this student website is presented as sample eFootball data.

Football Concepts

Attack

Attackers focus on movement, finishing and creating scoring opportunities.

Midfield

Midfielders connect defence and attack through passing and ball control.

Defence

Defenders protect their goal and stop opponents from creating chances.
